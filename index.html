ⓘ Sensor vs. Forecast

Have you noticed a difference between the Current Temperature and the Forecast High/Low? This is a normal and expected feature of high-accuracy meteorology!

1. Current Temperature (Real Sensors)
The current temperature displays live, real-time telemetry from the nearest official physical weather station (often located at low-altitude valley airports or coastal fields, e.g., --).
2. Forecast (Exact Coordinates Grid)
The forecast high, low, and daily cards are calculated for the exact geographic coordinates of your searched location, utilizing high-resolution grid models.

In coastal, mountainous, or complex terrain (such as Alaska or mountain foothills), a high-altitude searched location may be in a freezing snow shower, while the nearest physical airport sensor down in the valley reads a much warmer temperature.

TrueTemp integrates both systems to give you the most honest meteorological picture possible.
